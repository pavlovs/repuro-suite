## Customer + Service Profile

- **Regulatory moat:** Products are legally mandated (pre-cleaning step before sterilization). ISO 13485 certified, EU-MDR compliant. High barriers to entry for competitors.
- **Customer base:** 228 active customers across 18 countries (teaser: 400 in 56 countries L3Y). Mix of hospitals, OEMs (Getinge), and distributors (Healthmark, MRG).
- **Concentration risk:** Top 4 = 78% of 2025 revenue. Healthmark alone ~35% (EUR 569k), up from EUR 238k in 2023 — likely driven by STERIS acquisition. Single customer drives both the growth story and the risk.
- **No contractual lock-in:** Management meeting confirmed no long-term contracts, no minimum commitments. Relationships are long-standing (since 2016-2020) but entirely at-will. Stickiness comes from product quality and regulatory inertia, not legal obligation.
- **Revenue quality unknown:** The critical open question is whether products are consumables (annual replacement → genuinely recurring) or durable goods (5+ year cycle → structurally lumpy). This single answer changes the entire risk profile.
- **Growth pattern is lumpy, not linear:** 3yr CAGR of +11% is misleading. Excluding the 2025 spike, 2022→2024 CAGR was -1%. Revenue swings ±40% year-to-year driven by large customer order timing.

## Deal Commentary (updated 29.04 with Quantum Q&A)

- **Product lifecycle confirmed as consumable (2-3yr):** This was the #1 open question. 60% customer reorder rate within 3 years + wear-driven replacement = genuinely recurring demand, not lumpy project revenue. Significantly de-risks the revenue base.
- **Preliminary valuation range:** 3yr avg adj. EBITDA EUR 726k as basis. Broker midpoint is 4.8x (EUR 3.5M). With product lifecycle confirmed, multiple discount still warranted by concentration + volatility, but less than before. - **Earn-out likely necessary:** Healthmark/STERIS = 35% with no contracts, confirmed spot ordering. Revenue retention mechanism protects against post-closing customer loss.
- **Pre-closing conditions confirmed:** (1) GF loan EUR 810k — settlement confirmed ✅, (2) production premises — transitional use ok but relocation needed medium-term (new cost to model: rent + moving), (3) Hommel transition agreement per existing plan.
- **Q1-2026 is a negotiation lever:** -32% YoY AND Quantum now concedes 2026 will be at 2024 level. This undercuts the 2025 spike narrative and strengthens our position to bid at the lower end of the corridor. Use 2024 adj. EBITDA (EUR 623k) or conservative 3yr avg (EUR 726k) as basis, not 2025.
- **Remaining open question:** 2023 COGS spike (RFI 1) — not addressed by Quantum. Marginal for indication but useful for final pricing.
