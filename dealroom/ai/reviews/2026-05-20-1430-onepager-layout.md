# Onepager Layout Recommendation

Keep the 4-quadrant layout, but change Q2 into a two-layer panel: **Financial Overview** on top, **Valuation Summary** directly beneath it. This is the right hierarchy for a deal team because the reading order is: **what is the business, how does it perform, what is it worth, what happens next**. Valuation should not stand alone elsewhere on the page; reviewers read price in the context of EBITDA, margin and trend, not as an isolated fact.

The valuation block should be a compact horizontal strip inside Q2 with only 3-4 items: **EV range**, **EV mid / implied multiple**, **earn-out max**, and optionally **valuation basis year/date**. Make it visually stronger than today’s badges, but do not expand into the full waterfall. The detailed earn-out bridge belongs in the Valuation tab exactly as the owner requested.

Q3 should be reduced to **3-4 bullets maximum**:
- current stage + time in stage
- next concrete milestone
- main blocker / open issue
- target timeline or owner

Do not repeat valuation in Q3. That creates redundancy and weakens scanability.

So the page structure stays:
- Q1: Executive Summary
- Q2: Financials + condensed Valuation Summary
- Q3: Process / Status
- Q4: Service Portfolio & Customer Structure

This keeps the onepager usable across 10-14 active deals on a 1920x1080 screen, preserves drill-down discipline, and makes valuation visible enough for weekly pipeline review without turning the page into a mini model.
