Executive Summary
  • Cat is a specialized medical-equipment provider — distributing, installing and servicing equipment (barrier systems, surgical lights, sterilization / bedpan units in surgical rooms) for hospitals & clinics across Germany
  • Generated ~6.2 M€ revenue at ~0.93 M€ adj. EBITDA (~15%) in 2025; revenue CAGR 2023–25 ~39%; targets ~6.5 M€ revenue / 0.9 M€ EBITDA in 2026
  • Q1-2026: +10% revenue, +14% gross margin — supporting the sustainability of the 2025 step-up
  • ~70% of revenue from multi-year hospital projects (~95% of customers), plus ~24% from high-margin maintenance contracts (70–75% GM, growing)
  • EV at closing of 3.7 M€ (3.9x 2025 EBITDA); with anticipated earn-out total ~5.2 M€ (~5.5x); max earn-out tied to a 1.1 M€ EBITDA target¹
  • Could serve as an anchor platform — order backlog ~10.5 M€ across ~40 projects (85% of 2026B revenue already contracted), Germany-wide hospital base, roll-out potential across the Repuro portfolio

Service Portfolio & Customers
  • Revenue mix 2025: Projects ~71% (multi-year hospital projects), Service ~24% (maintenance / inspection, 70–75% GM), other ~5% — service share growing (from ~20% in 2023)
  • Order backlog ~10.5 M€ (~1.7x 2025 revenue) across ~40 projects; largest ~1.8 M€ over 3 years; 85% of 2026B revenue already contracted
  • Customer base ~95% hospitals & ambulatory surgery centers; Top-10 concentration ~50% (down from ~70% in 2023 — diversifying)
  • >40% of 2025 revenue from pre-2023 cohort customers; stable suppliers (Top-3 ~25–30% of procurement)
Process & Status
  • Status: LOI verbally agreed after phone call (01 Jul); to be sent before seller holiday (04 Jul), signing after holidays; key changes vs. previous offer: upfront multiple increased from 3.6x to 3.9x, earn-out threshold +25 K€, multiplier reduced from 6.0x to 5.5x — total anticipated EV unchanged
  • Transaction: 100% share deal, proprietary sourcing; shares 50/50 MD + silent partner; earn-out = 2 annual payments on 2026/2027 EBITDA; linear (EBITDA − 665 K€) × 5.5x; anticipated earn-out at BP (940 K€ EBITDA) = 1,513 K€
  • Transition: owner stays as MD until 2028, potentially a broader consulting / operational role (e.g., hospital project lead)
  • Real estate: office building & land (owned by Cat) to be sold to a separate investor with a long-term lease — a condition for closing
