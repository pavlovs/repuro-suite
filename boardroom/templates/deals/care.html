Executive summary
  • CARE develops and certifies own-brand clinical IT products (medical PCs, displays, power strips, isolators, iPad cases). CARE buys in parts and runs assembly, test and labelling in-house
  • Adds certification, MDR support and repair services for clinics and for manufacturers that build CARE components into their own products
  • 4.2 M€ revenue at 956 K€ adj. EBITDA (22.7%) in FY25/26 prelim. Revenue CAGR 2.4% and EBITDA CAGR 1.2% since FY22/23 after a dip in FY23/24
  • EV 4.4 M€ fixed (4.6x), up to 4.9 M€ (5.1x) with earn-out¹. Equity 4.0 M€ = 3.65 M€ cash at closing + 0.35 M€ vendor loan
  • Downside: FY25/26 adj. EBITDA is 40% above FY24/25, so the fixed EV equals 6.4x FY24/25 and 5.8x the FY23/24 to FY25/26 average. On flat prelim. adj. EBIT of 938 K€ the earn-out pays ~263 K€ (EV 4.7 M€, 4.9x)
  • Risks to test in DD: gross margin down from 49.7% to 43.7% over three years, FY25/26 figures from preliminary management accounts (BWA), 40% of purchases imported from outside the EU

Products & customers
  • Customers: clinics 50-60%, manufacturers ~30%, ambulatory the rest (owner estimate). Top-10 ~32%, no customer above 10%
  • Channel: direct, inbound only (no outbound sales today). Karl Storz, Dräger and Olympus as manufacturer partners. Tenders via Bechtle
  • Geography: by invoice address 73% Germany, 15% other EU, 12% non-EU. The owner puts total exports, incl. via partners, at ~30%
  • Team: 10 employees. Purchasing, sales and technology leads run day-to-day operations
Process & status
  • Status: indicative offer signed 13 Oct. LOI on the same terms sent 05 Nov. Owner lunch 10 Nov positive on both sides. Seller's advisor (PKF) closing its review
  • Transaction: 100% of the KG and GP shares. Locked box 30 Jun 2026. Shareholder accounts (1.1 M€) repaid within 12 months of closing. Property stays with the owner, lease of at least 5 years
  • Transition: owner leaves as MD at closing and advises for 12 months (28 h/week for 6 months, then 16 h). The operating MD is a joint choice. The second management level (purchasing, sales, technology) joins the future management team
  • LOI timeline: data room by 11 Dec. Final FY25/26 accounts end Dec. CDD/FDD by 05 Feb. Legal and tax DD by 05 Mar. Notary end Mar / early Apr 2027. Exclusivity to 23 Apr 2027 (seller pays 50 K€ on breach)
  • DD focus (agreed with Strada 28 Oct): FY23/24 dip, supplier concentration and chip sourcing, fixed cost base, OEM exclusivity clauses, why the seller accepts 4.6x
