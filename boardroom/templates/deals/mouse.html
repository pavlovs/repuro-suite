Executive summary
  • Mouse is a laboratory-equipment dealer, technical-service provider and niche instrument manufacturer. A ~50-year-old family firm in its 2nd generation (20 employees) that serves industrial (pharma, chemicals), public research and university-hospital labs
  • Sells equipment and devices to labs, follows with consumables, and adds technical service (maintenance, calibration, IQ/OQ) and in-house glass-equipment manufacturing (historical core)
  • Generated 3.9 M€ revenue (+14.5% YoY) at 0.65 M€ adj. EBITDA (17%) in 2025. Revenue broadly flat 2022-24 then a 2025 step-up. Effectively debt-free with net cash. 2023-25 revenue CAGR ~6%
  • Service: ~0.8 M€ (~20% of revenue), growing ~20% a year, recurring (maintenance, calibration, IQ/OQ), and it pulls through equipment sales. Service radius limited to ~100 km
  • EV at closing of 2.1 M€ (3.3x 2025A EBITDA), ~2.6 M€ (4.1x) incl. the anticipated earn-out of 0.5 M€¹
  • Strategic fit: same operating model as CARE (owner-led, asset-light, light assembly), ~2 hours from Berlin, no customer overlap with CARE

Service portfolio & customers
  • Revenue split 2025: Distribution ~75% (equipment, devices, consumables), Service ~20%, in-house products ~5%
  • ~30% of revenue from one-off project business (furniture, large parts) that leads to device sales
  • Medium concentration: Top-1 ~13%, Top-3 ~35%, Top-10 ~51% of 2025 revenue (top 10 in earlier years: ~38%)
  • By sector: ~50-60% industrial (pharma, chemicals, food), 30-40% research (universities, public research), <10% medical labs (owner estimate, not yet validated)
Process & status
  • Status: LOI signed 20 Jul (2.0 M€ upfront + earn-out up to 750 K€). On hold since Sep, when the Mantis seller withdrew. Exclusivity ended 26 Sep. On 17 Nov the owner confirmed he is still open to a sale and now involves his second management level
  • Next step: reconfirm terms and request 2026 YTD figures. Restart DD after the CARE LOI signature (data room, July DD material and 2025 accounts on file)
  • Transaction: 100% share deal, cash-free / debt-free, economic closing 01 Jan 2026. 2.0 M€ cash + earn-out. WC peg ~0.6 M€. Bilateral broker process (Everto). Owners (2 brothers, ~64) selling on succession
  • Transition (July LOI): both owners stay full-time to Dec 2027, then 50 to 80% as MDs to Dec 2028, successor from Jan 2027. Internal MD successor identified. Technical specialist to hire for the owners' technical role
  • DD focus: 2025 EBITDA sustainability (3-4 accounts drive the step-up), recurring vs. demand-driven revenue, successor and technical hire, buying-association dependency (IDL ~20% of COGS)
