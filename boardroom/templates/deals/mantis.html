Executive Summary
  • Mantis is a manufacturer-independent endoscopy distribution and service specialist serving gastroenterology practices, MVZs and hospitals, with own technicians plus refurbished-device and reprocessing-machine (RDG-E) offerings
  • Mantis generated ~3.2 M€ revenue at ~0.8 M€ adj. EBITDA (~27%) in 2025, with 2023-25 revenue CAGR of ~7%
  • EV at closing of 4.3 M€ (5.1x 2025A EBITDA), with anticipated earn-out of 0.5 M€ for an anticipated EV of 4.8 M€ (5.7x 2025 EBITDA)¹
  • Strong market position given resilient, largely cycle-independent demand in a niche with few competitors – additional tailwind given OEM repair exits and healthcare cost pressure
  • Mantis could serve as an attractive small platform given its differentiated technical / service capability, scalable model and untapped new-customer acquisition potential (especially driven by geographic expansion and partner companies network)

Service Portfolio
  • Manufacturer-independent endoscopy distribution and service partner for gastroenterology practices, MVZs and hospital endoscopy departments
  • Revenue from endoscope repair (own technicians), refurbished endoscope and peripheral sales, and RDG-E maintenance / hygienic validation / resale
  • Small, loyal customer base (100 active customers in 2025, 185 since 2022; top 3 = 27%, top 10 = 55%); long-standing, trust-based relationships; growth inbound / word-of-mouth, head of sales hired in April 2026
  • Sales largely driven by existing customers (~94%), multi-year NRR 90–102%; contractual recurring share is thin (~17% under annual contract) – customer interviews requested by the board before signing
Process & Status
  • Status: LOI signed; all DD reports final (FDD, TDD, LDD) — no red flags and no further indemnities arising; SPA, reinvestment term sheet and MD contracts walked through with the sellers 27 Aug and reworked (set-off against the earn-out only on final claims, 500 K€ paid out if the reinvestment does not materialise, junior MD carved out of pre-closing liability, financial warranties 3 years); seller's counsel review 04 Sep, feedback by 07 Sep; MD contracts to be signed before the notary so the closing conditions fall away — signing 14 Sep (Berlin notary), share transfer and closing expected in October
  • Upside under negotiation: exclusive distribution partnership for Germany with the Chinese manufacturer — not reflected in the LOI case; requires 250 K€ CAPEX into the product, which retains market value
  • Verified: change-of-control clause confirmed positive (no termination right on sale); still to verify before signing: volume case behind the investment and importer / product-liability coverage (MDR)
  • Transaction background: Acquiring 100% of shares (two managing shareholders: senior owner + son); proprietary sourcing, owner re-contacted Repuro after exclusive process was terminated due to personal fit with strategic buyer
  • Reinvestment: 0.5 M€ (~10%) of cash purchase price to be reinvested pari passu with investors in Repuro group – proving commitment of management to group vision
  • Transition: Senior owner/MD agreed to stay on for an extended period post-closing (beyond the initially planned 6-12 months); son (junior MD) already runs day-to-day and stays on for knowledge transfer – mitigating key-person risk
  • Due diligence: Specific focus on recurring vs. demand-driven revenue, OEM/Olympus dependency, adjustments, inventory valuation, customer churn, revenue visibility
