Phase II thesis Medical Technical Infrastructure (MTI): one supplier delivering devices, carts, IT and service to hospitals and ambulatory care as one documented system
  • Medical Technical Infrastructure (MTI) covers medical devices, IT hardware, carts, installation and service incl. regulatory documentation, about 2.7 B€ a year in Germany (mid case), with CARE as platform and the target search running
  • USP from the bundle: the hospital must ensure that the combination of devices, carts and IT is safe and wants one partner who documents the system. Small suppliers rarely carry that regulatory set-up, and the market is too fragmented for large players. Karl Storz, Dräger and Olympus build CARE's certified components into their own systems
  • Does consolidation make sense? Yes: no supplier combines devices, IT hardware, carts, installation and service for the hospital, and PE already consolidates service (H.I.G. with Medisolve)
  • How many actionable targets? 13 named candidates across carts, devices, service and infrastructure, four of them with 0.85-6.2 M€ profit in the latest filing. One contacted so far (section 04)
  • How do CARE and Mouse fit? CARE is the platform: its own products sit at the core of the offer. Mouse adds lab, an adjacent segment, as second seed of the starting platform
  • More details in section 02, thesis paper in progress
