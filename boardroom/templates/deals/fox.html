Executive Summary
  • Fox is a distribution and service partner for ambulatory care providers such as doctors, medical facilities, practice founders / operators, health centers, MVZs and ambulant surgery settings
  • Revenue stems from distributing medical products, and devices (such as ultrasound) with additional service offering (practice planning, IT, technical service, STK/MTK inspections)
  • LeasyMed is a newer rental / service offering with positive development and rising rental activity according to management
  • Fox generated ~3.5 M€ revenue at 0.42 M€ adj. EBITDA (12.1%) in 2025 (524 K€ LTM May-26), growing at ~6% CAGR 2023-25 — recovered after two weak years, current trading +12%
  • EV of 1.4 M€ at closing (3.3x 2025A adj. EBITDA) incl. the Co-Med share and the 100 K€ guaranteed earn-out (vendor loan, paid after the 2027 accounts); with the full anticipated earn-out of 0.65 M€ ~2.05 M€ (4.9x); equity value ~1.78 M€ (net cash 9 K€, net permitted leakage 280 K€)
  • Co-Med is the main logistics and procurement backbone of the business, enabling an asset-light operating model without own large-scale warehousing
  • Fox could serve as an attractive add-on with broad ambulant care customer access, differentiated service capabilities and further digital / service upside
