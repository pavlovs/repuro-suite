Executive Summary
  • Mouse is a laboratory-equipment dealer, technical-service provider and niche instrument manufacturer — a ~100-year, 4th-generation family firm (~20 FTE) serving industrial (pharma / chemistry), public research and university-hospital labs
  • Partners with labs from initial lab equipment & devices through regular consumables supply, complemented by technical service (maintenance, calibration, IQ/OQ) and in-house glass-equipment manufacturing (historical core)
  • Generated ~3.9 M€ revenue (+14.5% YoY) at ~0.6 M€ adj. EBITDA (~16%) in 2025; revenue broadly flat 2022–24 then a 2025 step-up; effectively debt-free with net cash; 2023–25 revenue CAGR ~6%
  • Service is the strategic engine — ~0.8 M€ (~20% of revenue), growing ~20%/yr, recurring (maintenance, calibration, IQ/OQ) and pulls through equipment sales; service radius regionally capped (~100km)
  • EV at closing of 2.1 M€ (3.3x 2025A EBITDA), with anticipated earn-out of 0.5 M€ for an anticipated EV of ~2.6 M€ (4.1x)¹
  • Strategic fit: operationally aligned with Repuro (B2B distribution & service) but in the adjacent lab vertical — limited overlap with the core medical-facility segment; attractive entry into the lab vertical on individual merit and valuation
